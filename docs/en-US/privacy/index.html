Paircast Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-02. Paircast is an invite-only private cloud sync player. It does not provide a public catalog and does not sell your data.

Information we process

  • Account and nickname: The account and password you enter are sent only to the self-hosted GO-IM server you configured, to sign you in. Your nickname is shown to members of rooms you join.
  • Avatar: If you choose a photo, it is compressed on-device and uploaded to that GO-IM server, which serves it to room members.
  • Chat messages: Messages are sent only to invite-only rooms you join and are delivered through your GO-IM server. Playback-sync signals are relayed in real time and are not stored.
  • Credentials: Object-storage keys and your GO-IM session token stay in on-device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage) so the app can access your own services. Shared configuration links are wrapped with AES-GCM instead of pasting secrets in plaintext.
  • Optional third parties: If you enter an OpenSubtitles API key or TMDB API Read Access Token, those queries go to the corresponding services.

What we do not do

  • No advertising tracking and no App Tracking Transparency advertising identifier.
  • No public film catalog, and your media is not distributed to users who were not invited.

Third parties

The self-hosted GO-IM server operated by whoever invited you, your S3-compatible object storage, and any OpenSubtitles or TMDB services you configure yourself. Their processing is governed by their own terms. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Deletion

In the app, open Me → Delete Account to clear your avatar, on-device configuration, and session. GO-IM accounts are created by the server operator; ask them to disable or remove the account itself.

Contact

Questions and reports: Support or GitHub Issues.
