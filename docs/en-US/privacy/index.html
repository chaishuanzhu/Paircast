Paircast Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14 (updated for version 2.0). Paircast is an invite-only private cloud sync player. It does not provide a public catalog and does not sell your data. You must agree to this policy and the Terms of Service before signing in.

Your data lives on the self-hosted server you connect to, which is run by its operator (usually whoever invited you); the Paircast developer has no access to it. That server has two parts: the Paircast service (accounts, media library, watch rooms) and its companion GO-IM messaging service (chat, avatars, stickers).

Information we process

  • Account and password: The account and password you enter are sent only to the Paircast server you configured, to sign you in. The server stores only a hash of your password.
  • Email: The server admin creates your account with your email address, which is stored on that server as your sign-in account. If the server has email set up, your initial password is sent to that address through the mail service its operator configured. There is no sign-up in the app.
  • Nickname and avatar: Stored by the GO-IM service and shown to members of rooms you join. If you choose a photo as your avatar, it is compressed on-device and uploaded to that server’s storage.
  • Chat messages: Messages are sent only to invite-only rooms you join and are delivered and stored by the GO-IM service. Stickers you send come from the sticker packs the server provides; your photo library is not uploaded.
  • Watch rooms: Room members, the current title and the host’s playback position are stored on the Paircast server so members stay in sync and late joiners can catch up. Idle rooms are deleted automatically (after 72 hours by default). Play / pause / seek signals are relayed in real time through GO-IM and are not stored.
  • Subtitles: A subtitle file you choose to share with the room is uploaded to the server so room members can load it.
  • Blocks and reports: The list of users you block is stored on the server and follows your account. When you report a message, its text (up to 500 characters), your and the other user’s account IDs, the room ID and the time are sent to that server. Only the server’s admins can see reports, and they use them to act on abuse (for example, banning an account).
  • On your device: The app keeps only the server address and your session token in on-device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage), plus a cache of images such as posters and avatars. Storage keys and third-party tokens stay on the server and are never stored on your device.
  • Media library: The movie list, playback links, subtitles and posters come from the Paircast server. Video streams load directly from the short-lived links it issues. If the server operator enables OpenSubtitles or TMDB, your subtitle searches and the movie titles in the library are sent to those services by the server; the app does not contact them directly.

What we do not do

  • No advertising tracking and no App Tracking Transparency advertising identifier.
  • No public film catalog, and your media is not distributed to users who were not invited.

Third parties

The self-hosted server (the Paircast service and GO-IM) run by whoever invited you, together with the S3-compatible object storage, mail service, OpenSubtitles, and TMDB services that the server operator configures. Their processing is governed by their own terms. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Deletion

In the app, open Me → Delete Account and enter your current password. The server permanently deletes your Paircast account (email, password hash) and your GO-IM user (nickname, avatar), and the app clears its on-device configuration, session, and caches. Chat messages already sent to rooms and report records are held by the server operator; contact them to have those removed. Admin accounts are handled by the server operator.
