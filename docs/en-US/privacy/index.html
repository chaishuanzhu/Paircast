Paircast Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03 (updated for version 2.1). Paircast is an invite-only private cloud sync player. It does not provide a public catalog and does not sell your data. You must agree to this policy and the Terms of Service before signing in.

Information we process

  • Account and nickname: The account and password you enter are sent only to the self-hosted GO-IM server you configured, to sign you in. Your nickname is shown to members of rooms you join.
  • Email: The server admin creates your account with your email address, which is stored on that GO-IM server as your sign-in account. If the server has email set up, your initial password is sent to that address through the mail service its operator configured. There is no sign-up in the app.
  • Avatar: If you choose a photo, it is compressed on-device and uploaded to that GO-IM server, which serves it to room members.
  • Chat messages: Messages are sent only to invite-only rooms you join and are delivered through your GO-IM server. Playback-sync signals are relayed in real time and are not stored.
  • Blocks and reports: the list of users you block is stored on the GO-IM server and follows your account. When you report a message, its text (up to 500 characters), your and the other user’s account IDs, the room ID and the time are sent to that server. Only the server’s admins can see reports, and they use them to act on abuse (for example, banning an account).
  • Credentials: The app stores only the GO-IM server address and your session token, in on-device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage). Storage keys and third-party tokens are kept on the GO-IM server, never on your device.
  • Media library: The movie list, playback links, subtitles, and stickers come from your GO-IM server. Video streams load directly from the short-lived links it issues. If the server operator enables OpenSubtitles or TMDB, your subtitle searches and the movie titles in the library are sent to those services by the server.

What we do not do

  • No advertising tracking and no App Tracking Transparency advertising identifier.
  • No public film catalog, and your media is not distributed to users who were not invited.

Third parties

The self-hosted GO-IM server operated by whoever invited you, together with the S3-compatible object storage, OpenSubtitles, and TMDB services that the server operator configures. Their processing is governed by their own terms. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Deletion

In the app, open Me → Delete Account and enter your current password. The GO-IM server permanently deletes your account record (email, nickname, avatar, password), and the app clears its on-device configuration, session, and caches. Chat messages already sent to rooms are held by the server operator; contact them to have those removed. Admin accounts are handled by the server operator.
