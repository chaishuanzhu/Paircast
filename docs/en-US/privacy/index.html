Paircast Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-24. Paircast is an invite-only private cloud sync player. It does not provide a public catalog and does not sell your data.

Information we process

  • Account ID and nickname: The IM user ID and nickname you enter are used to sign in to Tencent Cloud IM and to display your identity in rooms.
  • Avatar: If you choose a photo from your library, it is compressed and uploaded to the object storage you configured. An object key is stored in your IM profile.
  • Chat messages: Messages are sent only to invite-only rooms you join and are delivered through Tencent Cloud IM.
  • Cloud credentials: Object-storage and IM secrets stay in the on-device Keychain so the app can access your own storage and messaging services. Shared configuration links are wrapped with AES-GCM instead of pasting secrets in plaintext.
  • Optional third parties: If you enter OpenSubtitles or OMDb API keys, those queries go to the corresponding services. Douban and IMDb are not requested by default.

What we do not do

  • No advertising tracking and no App Tracking Transparency advertising identifier.
  • No public film catalog, and your media is not distributed to users who were not invited.

Third parties

Tencent Cloud IM, your S3-compatible object storage, and any OpenSubtitles or OMDb services you configure yourself. Their processing is governed by their own terms.

Deletion

In the app, open Me → Delete Account to clear on-device configuration, session, nickname/avatar objects. Pre-provisioned IM accounts that must be removed from the console should be handled by the operator.

Contact

Questions and reports: Support or GitHub Issues.
